Part 2 · Technology 1

CRT: a beam inside a tube

An electron gun sends a beam.

The beam makes the coating glow.

It draws the picture line by line.

CRT = cathode ray tube
Key wordselectron gunbeamphosphor coatingglass tube
Source: IEEE05 / 12

Part 2 · Technology 2

LCD: it controls light

A backlight produces white light.

The light is controlled by liquid crystals.

Colour filters create the colours.

“LED TV” = an LCD with an LED backlight
Key wordsbacklightpolarising filterliquid crystalcolour filter
Source: Samsung Display06 / 12

Part 2 · Technology 3

OLED: it makes its own light

Each pixel produces its own light.

A pixel switches off for perfect black.

OLED screens are used in many smartphones.

OLED = organic light-emitting diode
Key wordsorganicdiodecontrastthin
Sources: U.S. Department of Energy, ENERGY STAR07 / 12

Part 3 · Zoom in

A picture made of pixels

A picture is made of millions of pixels.

On an LCD, 1 pixel = red + green + blue subpixels.

The screen controls their brightness.

Key wordspixelsubpixelRGB (red, green, blue)brightness
Source: Samsung Display08 / 12

Conclusion

Three ways to show a picture

CRT

It uses an electron beam.

LCD

It controls light from a backlight.

OLED

Each pixel makes its own light.

The technology changes, but the main job stays the same: a screen shows us information.
